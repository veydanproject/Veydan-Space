## New

- Chat: requests from people you do not know now wait in a separate list, as plain text only, and never wake your phone.
- Chat: you can invite someone with a link, a code or a QR code, and they get in without waiting in requests.
- Chat: group owners can mute members for a set time, choose who may write, set quiet hours and check messages before they appear.

## Improved

- Chat: your contacts and chats come first in the list, and the history of a group loads when you open it.
- Chat: the blocked list shows names, and blocking a stranger removes their chat.
- Chat: the notices about group rules are now written in plain words.

## Fixed

- Chat: a group set to "managers only" no longer shows the message box to other members.
- Chat: the first message sent with an invitation code is no longer lost.
