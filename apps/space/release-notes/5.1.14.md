## New

- Sync: Veydan Cloud keeps your notes and passwords in sync between your devices, turned on with one button.
- Sync: a Recovery Key of 12 words brings your account and your data back on a new device.
- Sync: trusted people among your contacts can help you get back in if the Recovery Key is lost.
- Sync: a new device is linked with a code from a device you already use.
- Interface: the first start is a short guide through the language, the modules, the account and sync.
- Interface: the new Account page holds your plan, sync, devices and recovery in one place.
- Chat: messages from Veydan Cloud about your plan and recovery are shown as cards.

## Improved

- Sync: setting up sync or changing its passphrase no longer freezes the screen for a few seconds.
- Interface: a "Finish setting up" card reminds you of what was skipped at the first start.
- Sync: choosing another storage now moves this device to it: the app asks first, and nothing on the device is lost.
