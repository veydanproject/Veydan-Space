## New

- Chat: on Linux, notifications hide names and text while the screen is locked.

## Improved

- Interface: on Android, the system backup and phone transfer no longer copy the app's data.
- Interface: erasing all data now asks you to confirm it, with your unlock secret when the app lock is on.
- Sync: a deleted item no longer comes back, and a device that rejoins keeps what you changed while away.
- Sync: one damaged file in the vault no longer stops the rest of your data from syncing.
- Backup: a failed restore leaves your data untouched, and an older backup asks before it is restored.
- Browser: a profile whose proxy was deleted does not start until you choose another one.
- Browser: profiles behind a proxy no longer show your real address to sites through calls or DNS.
- Browser: the browser engine is checked before it is installed or updated.
- Chat: while the app is locked, chats can no longer be read or written.
- Chat: pictures on servers you do not know are downloaded only when you tap them.
- Tor: custom settings lines that could start programs are no longer applied.
- Passwords: copied secrets leave the clipboard after 30 seconds, and entry links open only web and mail addresses.

## Fixed

- Notes: text typed on the phone while a note was saving is no longer lost.
- SSH: keys protected by a passphrase now open, and moving files deletes only what was fully copied.
