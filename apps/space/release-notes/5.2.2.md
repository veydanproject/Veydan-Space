## Improved

- Calls: calls between two people now connect faster.
- Interface: the phone layout is redesigned: buttons, lists, headers, the navigation drawer, settings and the Cloud page.
- Notes: on a phone the format strip now sits above the keyboard.
- Passwords: the passwords and two-factor screens are redesigned on the phone.
- Interface: on Mac the app now opens without a security warning.

## Fixed

- Channels: "Paste" works again on Android and on a computer, in the sign-in and in the post editor.
- Channels: on iPhone the channel foot, media after the app was away and reactions work as they should.
- Chat: a voice recording now ends properly when the app goes to the background.
- Interface: on a phone a field no longer hides under the keyboard.
- Passwords: "Edit" and "Change key" open the right entry on a phone.
- Chat: on Android the gallery, the camera and saving a picture ask for permission again.
- Calls: on Android a call asks for its permissions again after the app was reopened.
- Chat: unblocking a contact no longer rolls back on your other devices.
- SSH: a server without file transfer says so at once; the terminal shows the exit code.
