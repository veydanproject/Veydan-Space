## New

- Chat: select text while writing to make it bold, italic, struck through, a quote or code, or to send it as Markdown.
- Chat: requests to join a group have their own page in moderation, with a count, cards and actions for many at once.
- Chat: paste a picture or a copied file into a chat, or drop files on it; on a phone, send pictures from the keyboard.
- Calls: on a computer, share your screen in a voice call too; the other person sees that it is a screen.
- Chat: add the server of your team from its link or card, and talk in private chats and groups that stay on that server.
- Chat: a phone can be woken for a private server; Veydan sees which private server woke it and when, never who wrote or what.

## Improved

- Chat: the moderation queue shows one card per author, with actions for all their messages and one search by text, name or key.
- Chat: muting, pinning, archiving and deleting a chat now reach all your devices.
- Chat: the list of chats says what was sent, a photo, a voice message or a video, instead of a file name.
- Chat: group admins see the message sent with a request to join, and you see the one you sent while you wait.
- Interface: thin lines between the rows of lists, clearer tags and new icons.
- Notes: on a phone, notes are a compact list with their tags, folder and time.

## Fixed

- Chat: your profile picture no longer disappears for others after a couple of weeks.
- Chat: a contact's picture now appears without restarting the app.
- Chat: a group you join by its link now appears on your other devices too.
