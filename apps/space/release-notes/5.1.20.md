## New

- Chat: select text while writing to make it bold, italic, struck through, a quote or code, or to send it as Markdown.
- Chat: requests to join a group have their own page in moderation, with a count, cards and actions for many at once.

## Improved

- Chat: group admins see the message sent with a request to join, and you see the one you sent while you wait.
- Chat: sending a request to join again while the first one waits updates its message.

## Fixed

- Chat: your profile picture no longer disappears for others after a couple of weeks.
- Chat: a contact's picture now appears without restarting the app.
