## Improved

- Interface: on Mac the app now opens without a security warning.

## Fixed

- Chat: on Android the gallery, the camera and saving a picture ask for permission again after the app's window was reopened.
- Chat: on Android a refused camera permission is now said instead of "the camera did not open".
- Chat: on Android "Don't ask again" for notifications is respected again.
- Calls: on Android a call asks for its permissions again after the app's window was reopened.
- Chat: unblocking a contact no longer rolls back on your other devices.
- SSH: a server without file transfer says so at once instead of waiting.
- SSH: the terminal shows the exit code when the shell ends with an error.
