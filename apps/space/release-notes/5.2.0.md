## New

- Chat: channels, to read and publish news with comments, reactions and pinned posts.
- Chat: channel sections as tiles with their own icon, photo and colour, and hidden sections for the team.
- Chat: posts read like articles, with a title, three lines and "Read more", and the whole post above its comments.
- Chat: an extended post editor with a preview and Markdown formatting.
- Chat: voice messages and video circles in channel posts and comments.
- Chat: posts and captions can be edited, and an edited post shows when it was changed.
- Account: one total storage quota for files, notes and chat media, shown on the usage card.
- Account: sign in to the Veydan website by scanning a code or pasting a link, and end website sessions.
- Chat: cards for website sign-in and purchases, with a copy button for the key.

## Improved

- Chat: the side panel of a chat or channel can be resized, and the width follows you to your other computers.
- Chat: channels are listed by their latest post.
- Interface: on the phone, the apps button moved from the bottom bar to the header of the chat tabs.
- Interface: a new scroll bar and a new proxy icon.
- Chat: a new look for message bubbles and reactions.

## Fixed

- Chat: videos in channels show a preview frame and play on the computer.
